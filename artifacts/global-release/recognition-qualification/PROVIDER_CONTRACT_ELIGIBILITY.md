## OpenAI safeguards and minimal legitimate guardian mechanism

[Under-18 API guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/safety-checks/under-18-api-guidance) calls for reasonable age-appropriate safety steps: disclosures, filters, monitoring/reporting and age assurance where needed. It also addresses ZDR before processing personal data below 13 or the applicable digital-consent age. Nearr continues excluding under-13 and users below its regional thresholds. Do not equate a minor's own AI permission with guardian permission.

Proposed minimal mechanism, **not implemented or approved**: a guardian acts through an independently authenticated adult channel; use a reviewed age/relationship verification method (email ownership alone is not proof of guardianship). Give the guardian the scoped provider/purpose/retention notice and record a server-verified attestation reference, subject account, guardian reference, jurisdiction, notice/version, verification method, granted/revoked timestamps and audit actor. Store an opaque verification result rather than identity-document images where possible. Neither the minor nor a client-provided flag may write approval. Guardian and user can revoke; worker rechecks both immediately before each external stage and retry. Expiry, changed provider/notice and account transfer require reassessment. Counsel/privacy owner must approve assurance level, retention and jurisdiction coverage before implementation is enabled.

The current database deliberately has no guardian grant path: `minor_ai_qualified` is constrained false and `account_ai_access_reason` denies non-adults even in a synthetically qualified region. Replacing that denial requires an additive reviewed migration and adversarial tests, not merely changing an environment variable.

`store:false` avoids ordinary response storage but does not mean zero retention. Standard abuse-monitoring logs can include content and are normally retained up to 30 days, with exceptions; approved ZDR has separate conditions and image-safety exceptions. Account-level evidence is missing. No school exception is asserted for this consumer product.
