# Ground-truth provenance and promotion rule

The private `dataset_labels_private.jsonl` is the only accepted-answer store. The public `dataset_manifest.jsonl` contains source evidence and offset-only answer spans, not label classes or expected places. A label's `provenance` states what the creator/post itself establishes and what a separately controlled source corroborates. Recognition output, autosave, missing Wrong Place feedback, and candidate retrieval are not ground truth.

Exact-single requires evidence that **this post depicts** the particular physical place, not merely that the named place exists. A same-brand branch needs branch-specific support. A multi label additionally requires every depicted/relevant place, approximate segments, and a distinction between depicted and mentioned-only locations. A source naming several alternatives is not a complete depicted set. Region-only, ambiguous, unverified, and known-negative are distinct outcomes; a generic scene is a known negative only after review establishes that no reliable exact physical place can be inferred.

Examples from the new source audit: the La Fortuna waterfall Short names the site and narrates its stair descent; the [site operator](https://www.cataratalafortuna.com/) and [Costa Rica tourism board](https://www.visitcostarica.com/planning-your-trip/itineraries/adventure-8-days) independently corroborate that access path. The Blue Bottle Kiyosumi Short has a branch tag and exterior cues; the [brand's Kiyosumi page](https://store.bluebottlecoffee.jp/pages/kiyosumi) identifies the address. By contrast, the Blue Bottle Shinjuku Short is still unverified because a [brand cafe list](https://store.bluebottlecoffee.jp/pages/cafes) establishes a branch's existence but does not tie generic interior footage to it. The Mallorca three-beach Short cannot be labeled `VERIFIED_MULTI` from its title alone.

These examples remain development **candidates**. Their source auditor is not an independent held-out reviewer, and no reproducible local frames have been retained. Before sealing, a separate human must inspect the original post and each label, verify aliases and depicted/mentioned roles, record the review decision with `independent:true`, inspect answer-bearing text and overlays for every enabled mask, and adjudicate source/place grouping. The seal command enforces the machine-checkable portion; it cannot replace human judgment.
