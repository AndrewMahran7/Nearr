# Ground-truth provenance

109 accepted cases carry HIGH confidence, complete reviewed visual evidence and a distinct adjudicating reviewer. Reviewers were separate agent contexts, not independent human annotators. They inspected complete 1 fps or 2 fps contact-sheet timelines, including opening, middle, ending and scene changes; this is not a claim of continuous audiovisual playback. Rapid events between samples remain a limitation. Case promotion required source-specific visual observations and independent place corroboration, not caption agreement alone.

Private labels retain original public source reference, exact accepted identities/aliases, depicted versus mentioned roles, approximate segments, source/place groups, complete-set decision, collector observations, independent public corroboration, review decisions and prior identity exposure. Coordinates are copied from actual official/map features or official-address geocoding with source and precision notes. No fabricated precision, address or citation is used to complete a set.

Old model outputs, autosaves and lack of a Wrong Place action are not ground truth. Historical proposals and superseded notes remain in the private audit trail. A source caption supports discovery and context; ambiguous generic footage remains a candidate even if an official venue with that name exists. Full-corpus and per-file hash audits are retained privately; source unavailability or changed bytes requires re-review.

The original 124-row `dataset_manifest.jsonl` and its split file remain the preserved source-history inventory. The current runnable corpus is the ignored `.local/recognition-gold-dataset/benchmark_manifest_private.jsonl`, `benchmark_labels_private.jsonl`, and `benchmark_splits_private.json`. These include full source evidence and private truth; do not commit them. A recovery copy and all collection overlays live in the external private evaluation folder.
