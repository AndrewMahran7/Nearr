# Complete multi-place collection

Accepted complete videos: **15**. Total distinct depicted place appearances across those sets: **61**. Each accepted video has whole-timeline inspection, approximate segments, depicted roles, aliases, source/place groups, independent set reconciliation and HIGH confidence. Coordinate gaps remaining in accepted multis: **0**. Exact-set scoring eligibility is restricted to accepted complete sets; candidates never enter that denominator.

Collections included restaurant/cafe itineraries, landmark montages, beaches and waterfalls. Opening scenes required special review: some replayed a later destination, while others added an unlisted place. Repeated scenes of one venue stay one target. A cafe's interior, cup/food close-ups and exterior are not automatically separate places. Adjacent landmarks may be separate targets when featured as discrete destinations; a palace's garden/interior remain one estate under the recorded granularity policy. Place appearances count unique accepted destinations per video, not every repeated shot.

Caption-only destinations, sponsors, nearby recommendations and relocations are recorded separately from depicted places. Historical branches retain the original depicted physical address; closed branches are not remapped to their replacement. Captions that number a repeated place twice or label the wrong landmark are counterevidence. Unresolved opening footage, exact beach boundaries, generic interiors and missing branch identity keep a montage outside strict exact-set eligibility.

The target is 30 complete videos; **15 remain**. No multi-place baseline was run. Detection accuracy, place precision/recall/F1, exact-set accuracy, extra/missed places and autonomous exact-set resolution remain unavailable. Per-place coordinates are representative map/address points with reference and precision notes, not camera positions or measured entrances. Natural-feature itineraries score their featured waterfalls, not an unidentified camera-side table/viewpoint; this granularity is explicit in the private review and is not a claim that every filming venue was identified.
