# Source and evidence index

The preserved `dataset_manifest.jsonl` records the original public source inventory. Public collection inventories under `collection/` and the historical audit under `../recognition-gold-existing/` preserve acquisition history. They are not the final runnable benchmark. Source-level disclosures restrict those records to development/calibration as documented in the private canonical manifest.

The current canonical manifest, full source captions, source MP4 hashes, timestamped original-pixel frames, acquisition methods/dates and exact source references are private. Independent place proof uses official venue/branch/park/tourism pages, directly inspected visual references, and map/address records where required. Private review packets preserve exact URLs and identity-exposure notes. No URL alone is treated as reviewed visual evidence.

See [media collection](MEDIA_COLLECTION_REPORT.md), [visual evidence inventory](VISUAL_EVIDENCE_INVENTORY.csv), [provenance](GROUND_TRUTH_PROVENANCE.md), [adjudication](LABEL_ADJUDICATION_REPORT.md), [ablation inputs](DESCRIPTION_ABLATION_REPORT.md) and [storage recovery](PRIVATE_STORAGE_RECOVERY.md). Full captions, video files, original images, private labels and source-to-inference mappings must not enter Git.
