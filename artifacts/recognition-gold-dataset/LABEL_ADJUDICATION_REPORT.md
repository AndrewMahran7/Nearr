# Label adjudication report

**Snapshot: 2026-10-09 19:37 UTC.** The read-only private canonical manifest and label store each contained 195 case records: 101 READY and 94 candidates. This is a moving snapshot for the root dataset maintainer to refresh before final publication. These reports give only aggregates and methods; per-case accepted places, complete captions, review packets, and media stay private.

All 105 historically exposed source references were attempted for media; 102 videos were acquired and 100 distinct historical records entered the deduplicated canonical overlay. Forty-five historical records are READY development cases (42 exact singles, two complete multis, one known negative); 60 remain candidates. None may be treated as a sealed holdout because earlier outcomes or research were exposed. The fresh negative batches contribute 33 YouTube and five TikTok READY cases after two review passes. The YouTube collection report publicly described its no-place candidate purpose, so those 33 are explicitly **development or calibration only**, even though their original posts had not previously been scored. The TikTok source-specific acquisition packets remain private; current canonical splits are development-only pending the holdout gates.

A first pass reviewed the full source text and full one-frame-per-second video sheets, recording all depicted segments, original-pixel answer overlays, and counterevidence. A separate agent reviewed the source and independent venue, operator, park, tourism, or other public identity evidence without opening the first-pass proposed label. For a READY exact label, the depicted site must be tied to **this video**, not just to a plausible hashtag, old Nearr result, generic brand, or an address where the venue exists. A multi-place label needs the complete depicted set and approximate segments; collaborators, comparisons, product origins, and caption-only mentions are recorded as non-depicted. A known negative requires affirmative review that no identifiable physical destination is justified, rather than mere failure to identify one.

All 101 READY records have at least two review-pass entries. The second pass explicitly records blind-to-first-pass and independent-review flags for 87; 14 earlier historical records name a separate reviewer but do not carry those explicit flags. The reviewers were separate **agents**, not independent human annotators. Review packets, complete source evidence, and provenance links remain private, and machine validation cannot substitute for human adjudication. This limits claims about sealed human-verified test quality. Earlier automatic saves, recognition output, and Wrong Place events were research leads only. Two provisional historical negatives were rejected when the full video or independent filming evidence contradicted them. Incomplete montages, uncertain natural sites, and a landmark that no longer exists were not promoted to present-day exact READY status merely because an old label named them.

Accepted exact labels preserve place groups so duplicate posts or separate shots of the same site cannot cross splits. Two historical complete-multi cases received an address audit covering seven depicted destinations at six distinct street addresses, using each venue's own public address page and the U.S. Census address geocoder. The two destinations in one plaza properly share a street coordinate but retain distinct place groups. Geocoder results are street-address estimates, not measured entrances or suite coordinates. Normal `visual_only` includes legitimate in-video text; a stronger scene-only flag is reserved for cases where distinctive visual geometry is independently corroborated without an answer-bearing pixel overlay.

For four READY cases, a separate private VISUAL_AUDIO proposal contains actual spoken-audio ASR transcripts with segment times and model/media hashes. Source-provided in-video subtitles and captions were cross-checked; direct acoustic listening was unavailable. ASR name errors and spoken mentions of non-depicted collaborators are called out in review notes. These transcripts add an evidence view, not new truth labels. Under the current view definition, `description_hidden` removes caption, description, and revealing hashtags while retaining authentic speech; `visual_only` removes the transcript and social metadata.

No candidate is promoted by a model prediction, by a public URL alone, or by a second reviewer merely agreeing with an exposed first-pass answer. Exact source access, permitted retained frames, full temporal coverage, source/place corroboration, complete-set review where relevant, and agreement on the private place groups are required before READY. The dataset is still short of its broader collection targets and has no sealed, independent human holdout result. No paid model or recognition API call was used for adjudication; **paid API spend was $0**.
