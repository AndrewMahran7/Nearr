# Instagram collection candidates

The companion `instagram_collection_candidates.jsonl` inventories 15 distinct public Instagram posts inspected on 2026-10-08 in a bounded browser research session. It contributes five multi-place candidates, outdoor beach/cliff/waterfall candidates, sparse-metadata cases, and a no-place product tutorial. These are **research candidates**, not scored gold cases: every row has `state: candidate` and `exposure: new_unscored`.

The collection contains public post URLs and IDs, short faithful caption excerpts (at most 25 words per post), source dates where visible, source-grounded categories, answer-bearing text offsets, and research geography. A caption excerpt is marked `excerpt_or_place_list`; it is not a full caption reconstruction. The few short complete captions are marked `complete`. The public manifest contains no expected place names outside the post evidence and no verification outcome. Provisional place hypotheses, source-specific research questions, and corroborating references are in the local ignored `.tmp/instagram_candidate_labels_private.jsonl`, keyed by opaque case IDs. They must remain private from model inference and are not sealed truth.

The reels were viewed in the public Instagram UI and representative scenes were visually inspected, but their media, still frames, and full transcripts were not retained. Thus `frame_paths` is empty and the four visual-dependent view flags are false for every row. `text_only` is true only for a complete retained short caption. Even those rows remain candidates pending an independent human pass. No visual ablation or full-post benchmark can run from public URLs or excerpts alone. On-screen place overlays were observed in several montages; `visual_answer_overlay: true` marks that future visual-only views will need overlay masking or exclusion.

Before promotion to `ready`, a reviewer should obtain permitted local frames, inspect the *entire* reel, record depicted versus merely mentioned locations and timed segments, resolve branches and neighboring landmarks, check source duplication/reposts, and corroborate exact places independently. The multi-place caption lists are not treated as complete depicted sets. Shared creator series are grouped by opaque `source_group_id`; the private label file holds provisional opaque place groups. Human review must set a defensible `complete_set_established` value and decision. No row from this batch was promoted to the heldout set.

The research used an existing authenticated session only to read publicly accessible posts. It did not like, follow, comment, message, post, download media, bypass login, or conduct bulk collection. The short excerpts and public references are intended as an inventory for rights-aware future capture, not a media archive.
