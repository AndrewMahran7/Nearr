# Historic outdoor media review

Ten previously inventoried public Instagram videos were acquired on 2026-10-09 UTC without cookies or account access. They total 118.5 seconds and 66,827,371 source bytes. Full-duration one-frame-per-second inspection covered 118 stills; fifty opening, intermediate, and ending JPEG frames were retained for benchmark views. Source and frame SHA-256 values were checked against the actual files. The compact JSONL and CSV inventories contain URLs, content IDs, hashes, durations, timestamps, counts, and acquisition status; they omit captions, private labels, and absolute media paths.

Six rows are `ready`/HIGH following an independent named-site/photo review and a final collector adjudication: Koosah Falls, Waimea Bay Jump Rock, two Tamolitch Blue Pool posts, and two Makapipi Falls posts. The two Tamolitch posts share a place group; the two Makapipi posts share another. Koosah shares a group with a previously accepted historical case. An old Ching's Pond claim for one Maui clip was false; the independent review identified the same Makapipi plunge pool in both Maui clips. Initial collector judgments were tentative and are retained as such in the private review history. Four clips remain `candidate` because the precise Mexican cenote, Italian cove, Vermont quarry, or forest river gorge is unresolved. Some source captions only specify a region or country; that metadata alone did not establish exact truth.
