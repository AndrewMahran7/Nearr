# Fresh public no-place candidate collection

On 2026-10-09, a bounded public YouTube search selected 40 previously unscored short posts in nail art, drawing, craft, and makeup. The collector attempted each source without signing in, using `yt-dlp` with a 720p video-plus-audio format and one retry. Thirty-three videos were retrieved and SHA-256 hashed. Seven media requests failed; those seven have no visual evidence and are excluded from the private case manifest. The compact [capture inventory](fresh_negative_capture_inventory.jsonl) records public source IDs and URLs, acquisition reasons, durations, frame counts, timestamps, and hashes. It does not contain full captions, local media paths, or labels.

For the 33 acquired videos, a first reviewer inspected the complete title and description and contact sheets sampling every second of the full 10–27 second source. Five full-resolution opening, middle, ending, and intermediate frames were retained and hashed for each case: 543 timeline stills inspected, 165 benchmark frames retained. Each clip appeared to be an object demonstration, drawing, craft, or presenter against a generic background. No identifiable physical destination was seen or named as the subject. The first pass proposed a no-place label for all 33, but all remain **candidates** until a different reviewer agrees after viewing the full source and description. Fifteen short, varied cases have been handed off in a source-only blind packet. Neither the first-pass proposal nor the private packet is ground truth or a sealed held-out set.

Full titles/descriptions, first-pass decisions, local videos, contact sheets, frame paths, and provisional labels live outside Git at `C:/Users/andre/Desktop/Nearr-gold-private-2026-10-08/fresh_negative_batch/` and `.../media/<case_id>/`. Media is temporary and scheduled for local retention through 2026-11-08. No spoken-audio transcript was collected; the benchmark evidence consists of the title, description, and retained video frames. The sample is concentrated in short YouTube tutorials, so later negative collection should diversify platform, duration, and subject before making broad performance claims.
