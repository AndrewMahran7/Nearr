# Fieldnotes native comparison

The evidence in `artifacts/fieldnotes-implementation` is captured from the actual Android framebuffer of a Pixel 8a AVD running the React Native development app. It is not an HTML rendering or an iOS screenshot. Each accepted PNG has a neighboring JSON with its route, data provenance, viewport, font scale, source commit and `visuallyVerified: true`.

The approved reference is the Fieldnotes Light winner at visual-direction commit `368f8e7`, particularly `mockups/winner/{map,selected,search,review}.png`. The generated `reference-vs-native` boards pair those references with accepted native images and record source hashes. Screen content differs because the implementation uses available saved records or explicitly named read-only fixtures, rather than inventing the reference's travel photography.

The final evidence set contains **58 unique accepted native captures**: 15 Light, 10 Dark, 10 small-device, eight at 1.6× text, seven at 2× text, and eight performance observations. Two additional files in `real-data/` are explicitly labeled provenance copies, not extra evidence. **20 reference-versus-native boards** are indexed in `reference-vs-native/manifest.json`; unavailable capture/reference combinations are listed as skipped. Two rejected startup/diagnostic pairs are retained separately under `rejected-captures/` and never counted as passes.

Native review directly led to the calmer Android light map style, a header fade that protects status text, clipping the translated Saved peek above the dock, a shorter font-aware peek, a compact no-photo Saved feature, removal of a duplicate welcome wordmark, and correction of the detail metadata separator. ## Size and text coverage

The small-device set uses a 750×1334 framebuffer at density 320, equivalent to 375×667 Android logical pixels. The map, selected card, real-name Saved list, photo detail, multi-place review, Quick Check, account and welcome are captured at font scale 1. Primary navigation and Save actions remain visible. Long Saved names wrap. Additional `android-onboarding-scrolled.png` and `android-photo-place-scrolled.png` frames verify that the welcome's remaining copy/provider chips and detail's source/reason content can be reached by scrolling. The detail sample does not establish reminder/visited interaction behavior.

The enlarged-text sets use font scales 1.6 and 2.0 on the normal 1080×2400/density420 viewport. Changing Android font scale during the running React Native process initially changed responsive branches but left some glyph metrics stale. Final enlarged-text captures were made after a cold app restart, with actual text enlargement visually checked. The 2× account title and Google action wrap over multiple lines; gallery thumbnails become a single accessible photo action and the detail actions stack. Secondary map summary and alternative-review subtitles can use ellipses, and the Saved search placeholder clips at 2×; these are recorded as remaining polish rather than hidden. Android animator duration scale was zero for this pass; still images do not establish motion quality or physical haptic response.

Only visually verified images are final evidence. Rejected white loaders, stale frames, Fast Refresh bars and LogBox overlays are excluded from comparison boards. iOS Apple Maps, iOS Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, physical-device haptics, and the iOS Share Extension still require installation/testing on an iPhone. The separately produced Development IPA establishes a native build result, not iPhone visual validation.
