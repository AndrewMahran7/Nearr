The new `20261009000006_share_job_notification_at_most_once.sql` migration separates a recoverable pre-send `sending` claim from an irreversible `notification_attempt_started_at` marker. A stale claim with no marker can be reclaimed with a fresh fenced attempt ID. A stale claim **with** a marker becomes `delivery_unknown` and is never eligible for automatic send again. The marker RPC rechecks current job state, payload type/job ID, logical ID, and attempt ID in one transaction before the worker calls Expo. The job result remains authoritative when notification delivery is unknown. Pre-send token-query failure may retry with a bounded budget; fetch errors, timeouts, resets, non-2xx responses, and malformed/partial Expo responses after request start do not retry. Explicit per-message permanent rejection terminates delivery. Neither immediate drain nor cron bypasses these rules.

The stable logical ID is `n1:<job UUID>:<c|h|f>` (41 ASCII bytes), distinguishing completed, needs-help, and failed events. It survives safe pre-send recovery; unrelated jobs, states, and Nearby alerts do not collide. Every Expo message carries it as `collapseId` and Android `tag`. Coalescing is defense-in-depth, **not** an idempotency guarantee. Returned tickets are associated with token-row, logical, and attempt IDs and submitted time. Receipts observe known tickets, record handoff/error metadata, and disable `DeviceNotRegistered` tokens; receipts never send. `delivery_unknown` is internal telemetry, not a user-facing result error.

Crash windows: (A) before claim: cron recovers; (B) claim before marker: stale pre-send claim recovers with old attempt fenced; (C) marker before HTTP: push may be missed, approved tradeoff; (D) request accepted/response lost: unknown, no resend; (E) ticket returned/DB write fails: metadata RPC retries three times without provider resend and may update a stale `delivery_unknown` row. If worker/DB fails before ticket persistence, the ticket can be lost and no receipt is possible; logs retain attempt ID and ticket count but not token/ticket secrets. This cannot safely be solved by resending; (F) submitted/process crash: no resend. Migration refuses to proceed with any legacy `sending` or `retryable_failed` row because those old states may have contacted Expo.
